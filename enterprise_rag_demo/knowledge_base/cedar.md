# Cedar — Vendor proposal

Fictional demonstration data. Quote date: 20 November 2026.
Vendor ID: cedar. All prices are INR, excluding tax. No discounts are quoted.

## Commercial terms
Monthly price per seat: INR 1,100.
One-time setup fee: INR 10,000.
Minimum billed seats: 5. Subscription pricing is monthly price times billed seats times months.

## Vendor-stated capabilities
Customer content and all backups are stored in India.
Role-based access and administrator audit logs with 180-day retention are included.
Login supports passwords and multi-factor authentication; SAML SSO is not supported.
Customer-data deletion is offered within 30 calendar days after termination.
Integrations include Slack; Jira integration is not documented.
The vendor offers a standard data processing agreement, but no signed agreement is in this knowledge base.

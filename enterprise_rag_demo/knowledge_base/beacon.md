# Beacon — Vendor proposal

Fictional demonstration data. Quote date: 20 November 2026.
Vendor ID: beacon. All prices are INR, excluding tax. No discounts are quoted.

## Commercial terms
Monthly price per seat: INR 750.
One-time setup fee: INR 35,000.
Minimum billed seats: 20. Subscription pricing is monthly price times billed seats times months.

## Vendor-stated capabilities
SAML SSO and role-based access are included.
Primary customer content is stored in Mumbai, India; disaster recovery backups are stored in Singapore.
Administrator audit logs are available with 90-day retention.
Customer-data deletion is offered within 30 calendar days after termination.
Integrations include Slack and Jira.
The vendor offers a standard data processing agreement, but no signed agreement is in this knowledge base.
India-only backup storage is not offered in this proposal.

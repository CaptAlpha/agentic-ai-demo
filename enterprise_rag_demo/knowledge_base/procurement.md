# Northstar Labs — Procurement policy v2

Fictional demonstration data. Effective 1 November 2026.
This policy supersedes procurement_legacy.md in full.

## Approval thresholds
All software subscriptions require the requesting department manager's approval.
Contracts with a total pre-tax commitment above INR 250,000 also require Finance approval.
Contracts above INR 500,000 additionally require the CFO's approval.
Total commitment includes all subscription months and one-time setup fees.

## Mandatory requirements
Tools processing customer data must support SAML SSO and India-only storage of customer content and backups.
Security review and a signed data processing agreement are required before purchase for customer-data tools.
Vendor claims alone do not constitute completed security review.
When multiple vendors meet mandatory requirements, compare full-term cost and operational fit.
If no vendor meets requirements, request a documented exception; never silently relax a mandatory requirement.

## Process
The requester prepares a vendor comparison and obtains required approvals before any order.
This assistant may draft a recommendation but cannot approve or submit a purchase.
The policy does not define a purchase order processing SLA.

# Northstar Labs — Expense reimbursement

These are fictional policies created for this demonstration.
Effective date: 1 November 2026.

## Submission and payment
Submit expense claims through the employee expense portal.
Attach an itemized receipt and the business purpose for each claim.
Claims must be submitted within 30 calendar days of purchase.
Approved claims are paid within 10 business days after Finance approval.

## Travel and meals
Domestic hotel reimbursement is capped at INR 6,000 per night before taxes.
Business travel requires manager approval before booking.
Meals during approved business travel are reimbursed up to INR 1,200 per day.
Alcohol and personal entertainment are not reimbursable.

## Internet
Remote employees may claim internet costs up to INR 1,000 per month.
Attach the monthly internet bill; this is separate from the equipment allowance.
Unused monthly internet allowance does not carry forward.

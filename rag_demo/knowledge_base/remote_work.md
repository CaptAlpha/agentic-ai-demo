# Northstar Labs — Remote work policy

These are fictional policies created for this demonstration.
Effective date: 1 November 2026.

## Work location
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval.
Tuesday and Thursday are collaboration days in the office.
Fully remote arrangements require written approval from People Operations.

## Equipment
New employees can claim a one-time home office equipment allowance of INR 15,000.
Eligible items include a monitor, keyboard, mouse, and ergonomic chair.
Equipment purchases require manager approval before purchase.
Submit itemized receipts within 30 calendar days of purchase.
Internet subscriptions are excluded from the equipment allowance.

## Working across borders
Working from another country requires People Operations approval before travel.
The policy does not specify a maximum number of overseas working days.

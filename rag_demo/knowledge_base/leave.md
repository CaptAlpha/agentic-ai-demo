# Northstar Labs — Leave and onboarding

These are fictional policies created for this demonstration.
Effective date: 1 November 2026.

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Submit planned leave requests at least seven calendar days in advance.
Manager approval is required before leave is confirmed.
Up to five unused annual leave days may carry forward into the next year.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 10 days of sick leave per calendar year.
Notify your manager as soon as practicable when taking sick leave.
A medical certificate is required for absences longer than three consecutive days.

## Onboarding
Complete security training within the first seven calendar days of joining.
Enroll in multi-factor authentication before accessing company systems.
Your manager assigns an onboarding buddy during your first week.
